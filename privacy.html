Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy covers the Buch Buys Purchase Tracker ("the app"), a private tool operated by BBuys LLC for its own bookkeeping. The app has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • Order confirmation and receipt emails from retailers, in email accounts owned by BBuys LLC.
  • Our own QuickBooks Online company, using the access we grant when we click "Connect to QuickBooks". The app reads the chart of accounts and vendor list, and creates expenses, vendors and receipt attachments.
  • Our own seller accounts on eBay, Amazon and Walmart, using the access we grant when we connect each one. The app reads our sales and refund amounts, marketplace fees, payouts, item titles, SKUs, quantities and listing stock levels.

What the app does not collect

The app does not store buyers' names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses or payment details from eBay, Amazon or Walmart. It uses no cookies or tracking and collects nothing from website visitors.

Where data is stored

All data stays on BBuys LLC's own computer. Passwords and connection tokens (QuickBooks, eBay, Amazon, Walmart) are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection. The app has no server and no cloud database.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data is sent only to QuickBooks Online (Intuit) at our request.

Disconnecting

We can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from the app's Settings page or from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps). eBay, Amazon and Walmart access can be revoked at any time from each marketplace's account settings. After disconnecting, the app can no longer access that account.
