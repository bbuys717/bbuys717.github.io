Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy covers the Buch Buys Purchase Tracker ("the app"), a private tool operated by Buch Buys LLC for its own bookkeeping.

What the app accesses

  • Order confirmation and receipt emails from retailers, in email accounts owned by Buch Buys LLC.
  • Our own QuickBooks Online company, using the access we grant when we click "Connect to QuickBooks". The app reads the chart of accounts and vendor list, and creates expenses, vendors and receipt attachments.

Where data is stored

All data stays on Buch Buys LLC's own computer. Passwords and QuickBooks connection tokens are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection. The app has no server and no cloud database.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data is sent only to QuickBooks Online (Intuit) at our request.

Disconnecting

We can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from the app's Settings page or from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps). After disconnecting, the app can no longer access QuickBooks, and the stored connection token is removed.
