Buch Buys Purchase Tracker

The Purchase Tracker is a private tool used internally by Buch Buys LLC. It runs on our own computer and records the inventory we buy for resale, and can send those purchases to our own QuickBooks Online company as expenses.

It is not offered to the public.

Already using it? Open the Purchase Tracker on your PC (http://localhost:5055), then go to Settings → QuickBooks.
